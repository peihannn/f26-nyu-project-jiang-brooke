02 / Moonlight

月光似霜

床前明月光，
疑是地上霜。

Moonlight rests on the floor, pale as frost.

TOUCH THE LIGHT

03 / Look up

举头望月

举头望明月。

He raises his head. The same moon shines over home.

FOLLOW THE MOON

04 / Family memory

家人团聚

A lantern glows above a family gathered around the table.

Imagined memory of home; not a literal event stated in the poem.

LINGER A MOMENT

05 / Friends memory

朋友相聚

In a moonlit courtyard, friends share wine and poetry.

Imagined memory of companionship; not a literal event stated in the poem.

LET THE MEMORY FADE

06 / Return and reflection

低头思故乡

Back in the quiet room, he lowers his head.

床前明月光，
疑是地上霜。
举头望明月，
低头思故乡。

REFLECT

What brings home to mind for you?

Notice the journey from solitude, through remembered warmth, back to longing.
